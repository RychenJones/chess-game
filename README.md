## MVP Acceptance Criteria

The minimum viable version of this project must:

1. Build and launch successfully.
2. Display an 8×8 board with the pieces in their starting positions.
3. Allow players to select and move pieces according to their basic movement rules.
4. Reject invalid moves, including moving off the board, moving onto a friendly piece, or jumping over pieces when the piece cannot jump.
5. Allow players to capture opposing pieces and remove them from the board.
6. Alternate turns between White and Black.
7. Clearly indicate the selected piece and current player.
8. Remain playable after captures without requiring a winning condition.
9. Allow the game to be reset or restarted.
10. Document how to build, run, and play the game.

Check, checkmate, king safety, promotion, castling, en passant, AI, and networked multiplayer are outside the MVP scope.
